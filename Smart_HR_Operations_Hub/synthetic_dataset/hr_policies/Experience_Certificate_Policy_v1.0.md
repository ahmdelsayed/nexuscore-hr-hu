# NexusCore Technologies - Experience Certificate Policy
- **Document ID:** POL-CRT-004
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
- **Classification:** Internal HR Policy

## Section 1: Eligibility Criteria
Experience certificates are granted to both current employees and former employees who have completed a minimum of six (6) months of continuous active employment at NexusCore Technologies. Employees terminated for gross misconduct or under active probation (< 6 months) are not eligible.

## Section 2: Issuance Authority and Human Approval
Experience certificates are official legal instruments. No certificate may be generated, released, or signed automatically by AI systems. Every certificate requires explicit verification and digital approval by an authorized HR Manager (e.g. Sarah Nabil or People Operations Lead).

## Section 3: Verified Template Fields
The certificate must contain exclusively verified records retrieved from the official HR database:
1. Certificate ID (e.g., CERT-YYYY-XXXX)
2. Issue Date
3. Employee Full Legal Name
4. Verified Job Title
5. Department
6. Verified Start Date
7. End Date (or 'Present' for active staff)
8. Name and Signature of Authorizing HR Officer

## Section 4: Processing Time and Delivery
Approved certificates are processed within three (3) business days of request submission. The final branded certificate is issued as a tamper-evident PDF uploaded to secure, restricted storage with a time-limited download link sent to the applicant's verified email.
