# NexusCore Technologies - Leave Policy
- **Document ID:** POL-LEV-002
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
- **Classification:** Internal HR Policy

## Section 1: Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time permanent employees are entitled to twenty-one (21) paid annual leave days per calendar year. Employees with more than ten (10) years of continuous service or aged 50+ are entitled to thirty (30) days.

## Section 2: Sick Leave and Medical Certification
Employees are granted up to twelve (12) days of paid sick leave annually. Any medical absence exceeding two (2) consecutive calendar days requires an official medical certificate signed by an accredited healthcare provider submitted within 48 hours of return.

## Section 3: Emergency and Unpaid Leave
Employees may take up to five (5) emergency leave days annually, deducted from their annual leave balance. Unpaid leave requires written authorization from both the Department Head and the HR Director.

## Section 4: Leave Approval Workflow
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least five (5) working days in advance for annual leave. Direct managers must approve the request before HR records the debit. Emergency leaves must be submitted immediately upon return.
