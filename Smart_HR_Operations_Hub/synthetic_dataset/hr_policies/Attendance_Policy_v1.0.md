# NexusCore Technologies - Attendance & Punctuality Policy
- **Document ID:** POL-ATT-003
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
- **Classification:** Internal HR Policy

## Section 1: Core Working Hours and Attendance Logging
The standard workday starts at 09:00 AM. Employees must check in using the digital biometric/portal attendance system. A grace period of fifteen (15) minutes is permitted without deduction.

## Section 2: Tardiness and Grace Period Handling
Arrival between 09:16 AM and 09:45 AM is marked as Tardy. More than three (3) tardy arrivals in a single calendar month without prior manager notification results in a deduction of a quarter-day's salary.

## Section 3: Unexcused Absence
Failure to report to work or notify management for two (2) consecutive days is classified as Unexcused Absence. After five (5) consecutive unexcused days, HR begins termination procedures pursuant to national labor regulations.

## Section 4: Overtime and Working on Off-Days
Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the Department Manager. Approved overtime is compensated at 1.5x regular hourly rate for weekdays and 2.0x for weekends and public holidays.
