# NexusCore Technologies - General HR Policy
- **Document ID:** POL-GEN-001
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Effective Date:** 2026-06-01
- **Classification:** Internal HR Policy

## Section 1: Working Hours and Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to work 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday, between 09:00 AM and 05:00 PM. Professionalism, mutual respect, and data confidentiality must be maintained at all times.

## Section 2: Remote Work Policy
Under revised version 1.1, eligible full-time employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week (increased from 2 days) upon manager approval. Core team hours between 10:00 AM and 03:00 PM require active availability on company communication channels.

## Section 3: Communication and Request Channels
All formal employee requests regarding payroll, benefits, contract status, or grievances must be submitted via the Smart HR Portal or directed to hr-ops@nexuscore-tech.com. Informal verbal requests are not officially recognized.

## Section 4: Grievance and Escalation Procedure
In the event of workplace concerns, employees should report directly to their People Operations Partner. If unresolved within 5 business days, the matter escalates to the Chief People Officer.
