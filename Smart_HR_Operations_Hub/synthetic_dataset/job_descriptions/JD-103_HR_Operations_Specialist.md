# Job Posting: HR Operations Specialist
- **Job ID:** JD-103
- **Department:** People Operations
- **Location:** Cairo, Egypt (On-site / Hybrid)
- **Status:** Open

## Job Summary
We are looking for an organized and detail-oriented HR Operations Specialist to manage end-to-end employee onboarding, compliance, records management, and operational HR reporting.

## Technical Requirements (Required Skills)
- HRIS administration and data governance
- Egyptian Labor Law compliance and employment documentation
- Talent Acquisition lifecycle coordination and applicant tracking
- Employee Onboarding and documentation auditing
- Advanced Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets reporting and dashboarding
- Effective business communication and grievance handling

## Preferred Qualifications
- HR Certification (SHRM, HRCI, or equivalent)
- Minimum 3 years of hands-on People Operations experience
